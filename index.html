Our Heritage Story

For generations, Sri Lankan cuisine has been a celebration of culture, family, and tradition. At Ape Kama, we preserve these treasured recipes using fresh local ingredients, traditional cooking techniques, and the rich flavors passed down through generations.

From handcrafted Pittu and aromatic Leaf Rice to authentic Bamboo Rice and village-style desserts, every dish tells a story of Sri Lanka's culinary heritage.
